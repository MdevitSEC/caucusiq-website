Why CaucusIQ

Your list gets better every time you knock.

County files often lag behind real life, and most have few phone numbers. CaucusIQ keeps what your volunteers learn at the door.

Saved, even without signal

Results stay on the phone until it reconnects, then send themselves. Mark "not home" and "left literature" in one tap.

What volunteers learn stays put

When a canvasser finds someone has moved or passed away, the committee reviews it and the voter is marked on every list. A new county file won't erase it, and CaucusIQ flags any new activity for your review.

Lists that grow

Phone numbers and emails collected at the door go to a committee member to approve, then onto the voter's record. Each canvass can lengthen your next call list.

A tool you control

Your committee decides who gets a login and what each person can see. A new volunteer can be knocking doors the day they sign up.

For every candidate you endorse

Give your endorsed candidates their own login for their district. They start with everything your volunteers have learned at those doors, and their canvass adds to it.

Our rural pledge

Small places do the hardest organizing with the fewest hands.

If your county has fewer than 10,000 registered voters, counting every party, your committee pays what it costs us to run CaucusIQ for you, and nothing more. No application: we check your county's count against the state's enrollment totals.

Your data, plainly

Each committee's data lives in its own walled-off section of our database. Here's who owns what.

The voter file

A public record your committee gets from its Board of Elections. We process it for you, for election purposes only.

Everything you add

Notes, canvass results, lists, turf and tags belong to your committee. Ask and we'll send you a copy of all of it.

Your people

Members' and volunteers' details stay with your committee. We never contact them or market to them.

If you leave

If you leave with your bills paid, we send you a full copy, voter file included. We keep your data for 90 days in case you change your mind, then delete it.
