Privacy

Last updated October 29, 2026

This page covers caucusiq.com and the email you send us from it. The CaucusIQ app that committees use, and the voter data in it, is covered separately in the agreement each committee signs.

What we collect

Only what you choose to email us, usually your name, your email address, your committee and its county. This site has no forms, no sign-up, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. Our host, GitHub Pages, keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the site.

How we use it

To answer you, set up a demo and follow up on what you asked about. Because CaucusIQ is only for Democratic committees and progressive organizations, we may also use what you tell us about your committee to confirm it qualifies. We don't add you to a mailing list.

Who sees it

We don't sell, rent or trade it. Your email is stored with our email provider. We share it with no one else unless the law requires us to.

Deleting it

Write to privacy@caucusiq.com and we'll delete your emails and tell you when it's done.

Changes

If this page changes, the new version goes here with a new date.

Questions

Email privacy@caucusiq.com.
